About North Star Bakery

A neighborhood bakery built one loaf at a time.

Our story

Rosa Lindqvist began baking bread in her apartment kitchen and selling it from a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. Her neighbors kept asking where they could buy it the rest of the week, so in 2012 she opened a small corner shop on Larkspur Avenue.

The name came from her grandmother, who told her to always bake toward something steady, like the North Star. Fourteen years later, we still shape every loaf by hand, still use the same sourdough starter, and still know most of our customers by name.

Today we are a team of nine bakers, decorators, and counter staff who believe a good bakery should feel like part of the neighborhood.

Where our ingredients come from

Good bread only needs a few ingredients, so each one has to be worth it. We buy as close to home as we can and pay fairly for it.

  • Regional flour

    Our wheat and rye are grown and stone-milled within 200 miles of the shop. Fresh-milled flour tastes nuttier and keeps more of the grain's nutrition.

  • Local dairy and eggs

    Butter, cream, and eggs come from two family farms we visit every season.

  • Fruit in season

    Our danishes and cakes follow the farmers market: rhubarb in spring, berries in summer, apples and pears in fall.

  • Nothing wasted

    Day-old bread goes to the community fridge two blocks away, and our kitchen scraps go to a local compost program.

Behind the scenes

Ever wonder what happens before we unlock the door? Watch an early morning in our kitchen, from mixing and shaping dough to the first loaves coming out of the oven.
