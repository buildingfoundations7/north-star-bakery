Welcome to North Star Bakery

We've been baking for this neighborhood since 2012. Every loaf, croissant, and birthday cake leaves our ovens the same day it's sold, shaped by hand by people who live just down the street.

Stop in on your way to work, bring the kids by for a cookie, or let us handle the bread for your next gathering.

What we're known for

  • Slow-fermented sourdough

    Our loaves rise for up to 36 hours, which gives them a crackly crust and a tender, tangy crumb.

  • Morning pastries

    Butter croissants, cardamom knots, and seasonal fruit danishes, ready by 6:30 a.m. for the commuter crowd.

  • Celebration cakes

    Layer cakes for birthdays, showers, and office parties, decorated to order with 72 hours' notice.

Featured item of the week

Brown butter chocolate chunk cookies
